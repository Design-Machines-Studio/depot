`rejectedScope` list. The scorer checks the selected scope, requires all five
unsupported scope IDs, and rejects a scope that is both selected and rejected.
It no longer rejects words found anywhere in a free-text chunk title. The case
and prompt are revision 3; the suite is revision 2; the scorer is revision 3;
normalizer and behavioral contract are unchanged. Evidence bindings were
regenerated. Earlier result files were preserved outside Git.

Regression fixtures distinguish a safe selection that explicitly excludes
“Issue #86 Floor observation schemas,” actual selection of unsupported Floor
work, contradictory selected/rejected scope, missing scope fields, and malformed
JSON. The historical wording check is a scorer regression fixture, not a fresh
model result.

The native receipt still preserves requested identity separately from served
identity. Missing, contradictory, or fallback-unknown telemetry remains
non-comparable. Missing/malformed CLI telemetry has a native infrastructure
failure kind and remains an operational transport limitation with
`modelConclusion:null`. Native identity fixtures cover missing identity,
missing fallback status, inconsistent identity, explicit fallback, and valid
identity plus explicit no-fallback evidence.
